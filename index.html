About WorkHub

WorkHub runs four escape rooms in an old house at the end of Cemetery Lane. Each room has its own story, its own puzzles and its own reason to make you look over your shoulder.

You play as a team of 1 to 5 players. Lock in, find the clues and get out before the time runs out. Our game masters watch every room, so help is never far away – even when it feels like it.

How it works

  1. Choose an experience
